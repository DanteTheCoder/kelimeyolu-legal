Kelime Yolu – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Kelime Yolu apps for Android and iOS

1. Who is responsible

Kelime Yolu is developed by Talha Kekik, who is the controller for the processing described here.
Contact: talhakekik@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email or any other contact details.
  • We do not operate our own servers. Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads are only personalised if you agree in the consent form.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves your progress (solved puzzles, unlocked cities, daily streak), your coins and power-ups, your purchases (for example the ad-free status), and your settings (language, theme, sound, vibration, reminders). This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it with “Reset Progress” in the app settings, or by uninstalling the app. Android may include it in your own device backup if you have backups turned on.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, it shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. When an ad is requested, Google may process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID, or Apple’s IDFA if you allow tracking on iOS),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (views, taps).

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are loaded. If you do not consent, Google shows only non-personalised ads, which still use limited data for delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → “Privacy options”. On iOS, the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, the IDFA is not shared.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised ads and access to device storage; our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads. Buying the starter bundle or lifetime premium removes ads. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which product you bought, so it can unlock it. The privacy policies of Google and Apple apply to the payment. Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using the system rating dialog of Google Play or the App Store. The rating is handled by Google or Apple. We do not receive any personal data from it.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily puzzle reminder on your device. Reminders are created locally; no push server and no personal data are involved. You can turn them off in the app settings or in your device settings.

8. No analytics or tracking by us

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. Google or Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example installs or crash counts) if you have allowed this in your device settings.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in countries outside the EU, including the USA. Google LLC and Apple Inc. are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, restrict or object to its processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Because we do not hold any data about you on our side, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, and payment data to Google or Apple; we will gladly help, so just email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in your country of residence.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address, with its effective date at the top.
